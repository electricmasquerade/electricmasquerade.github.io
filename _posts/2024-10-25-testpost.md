I recently finished a CO2 Racecar using Fusion and generative design tools. 

![CO2 Racer](/images/co2_car_2.webp)

### The Design Process
I started by sketching out the design and then moved on to creating a 3D model in Fusion 360. I used generative design tools to optimize the design for weight and strength.

### Tools Used
- Fusion for 3D modeling
- Cura for slicing
- PLA filament for printing
